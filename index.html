How a trade works

From enquiry to vessel, step by step.

Every deal follows the same eight steps, with documents and payment running through the bank.

  1. Enquiry

    You send product, spec, quantity, destination port and delivery window (LOI / RFQ).

  2. Firm offer

    FCO with price, specs, Incoterm, laycan and payment terms.

  3. Samples

    Sample and lab analysis shared before contract where required.

  4. Contract

    Sales contract signed; buyer opens the agreed LC or pays the advance.

  5. Stocking

    Material sourced, crushed, sized and stocked at the loading port.

  6. Inspection

    Independent pre-shipment inspection by SGS, Intertek, BV or your nominee.

  7. Loading

    Vessel or containers loaded and the Bill of Lading issued.

  8. Payment

    Full document set presented through the bank; payment released.

Why buyers do not go direct

Why buy through one trader instead of going direct to a mine.

01

Supply assurance: no single-mine dependency

Going directYou are tied to one pit. If it stops for monsoon, labour or a regulatory halt, your vessel waits and demurrage runs.
Through one traderSupply is drawn from more than one source for each mineral. If a lot fails QC, material is switched to another source and your contract stays intact.
Your benefitOne contract and a steadier supply for your plant.
02

Quality that is certified, not just claimed

Going directA mine-gate lab figure and no bulk sampling. The cargo can differ from the paper when it arrives.
Through one traderJoint sampling, lab analysis and pre-shipment inspection by SGS, Bureau Veritas, Intertek or the agency you name. Loading is followed with photos and video. A lot that fails the agreed specification is not shipped.
Your benefitYou pay against certified results, with fewer surprises at the discharge port.
03

Export documents and payment, done to the letter

Going directMany mines are unfamiliar with LC clauses, bills of lading and discrepancy rules, and ask for full advance payment.
Through one traderPayment through bank channels: LC at sight, usance LC or documents against payment. Documents are prepared to your LC terms: invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, inspection certificate, fumigation where required, bill of lading.
Your benefitFewer discrepancies, and your money moves through the bank.
04

Port, logistics and customs: the hidden costs

Going directMine price is only part of the cost. Inland transport, weighbridge loss, storage, handling and clearance are arranged separately, each with its own margin.
Through one traderThese steps are coordinated by one person, and the quotation states what is included. You see the landed cost before you commit.
Your benefitA clear FOB quotation instead of bills that appear after shipment.
05

Market knowledge: the right grade, not the highest grade

Going directA mine will usually offer its best grade at its best price, and you may pay for purity your process does not need.
Through one traderTwenty years across iron ore, quartz, bauxite, silica sand, coal and steel. Grade is matched to your application, whether glass, ceramics, foundry or ferro-alloy, and to the freight and timing of the market.
Your benefitA sourcing adviser as well as a supplier, helping you avoid over-specifying.
06

One point of accountability

Going directAfter payment, updates depend on whoever answers the phone at a remote site.
Through one traderYou deal with one person, directly on WhatsApp or phone. You get an update at each stage: material at yard, sampling, loading, vessel berthing and documents.
Your benefitYou run your plant. One contact runs your supply chain.

Direct mines sell you material. A trader delivers a managed supply chain: sourced, inspected, documented and shipped through one accountable contact.
